Eldritch Studios

Video Game Studio Introduction

Project Overview

Eldritch Studios is a video game studio concept developed to explore cinematic logo introduction and tone-setting through motion. The project focuses on establishing atmosphere, pacing, and identity in a short-form logo reveal intended for use in game intros, trailers, and studio branding moments.

My Contributions

Designed the Eldritch Studios logo and visual identity foundation.

Developed the motion concept, pacing, and reveal structure.

Directed the logo animation to emphasize atmosphere and tone.

Selected typography, timing, and visual rhythm to support a game-studio introduction.

Assembled and delivered a finalized 30-second motion logo sequence.
