Mythos Book Store

Online Retail Book Store

Project Overview

Mythos Book Store is a conceptual online retail platform dedicated exclusively to weird fiction spanning horror, fantasy, sci-fi, and the uncanny. The objective of this project was to design an immersive digital storefront that reflects the atmospheric depth and symbolic richness of the genre itself.

Rather than relying on conventional e-commerce aesthetics, the visual system embraces high-contrast palettes, mythological motifs, and stylized illustration to create a cohesive narrative environment. The interface balances mood and usability, ensuring that while the experience feels immersive and thematic, navigation and product discovery remain intuitive and structured. The result is a fully realized UI concept that merges brand identity with digital functionality, positioning Mythos as a curated destination rather than a generic online bookstore.
