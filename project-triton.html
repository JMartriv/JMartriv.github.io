Triton Energy

Hydroelectric Infrastructure Branding

Project Overview

Triton Energy is a hydroelectric renewable energy company centered on generating clean, reliable power through water-driven infrastructure. This project focuses on building a brand system that feels both timeless and operational — merging mythic symbolism (Triton's trident and oceanic iconography) with the clarity and precision required for industrial environments.

The identity was designed to extend beyond traditional corporate branding into infrastructure applications, including facility signage, equipment labels, serial plates, inspection tags, and on-site staff PPE. The result is a cohesive visual system that supports a modern utility brand while remaining grounded in real-world functionality.

Created visual rationale and brand storytelling to connect the identity to hydroelectric power and Triton symbolism.

Designed branded collateral and real-world mockups, including PPE, business stationery, ID assets, and facility applications.
