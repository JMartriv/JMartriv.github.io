BORUKEN CATERING
FOOD DELIVERY SERVICE STATIONERYS
Project Overview
Boruken Catering is a family-owned Puerto Rican catering business serving the South Florida community. The client requested a visual identity that felt warm, welcoming, and culturally rooted, something that honored tradition while remaining polished and professional.

The objective was to develop a stationery and menu system that reflected the authenticity of home-style Puerto Rican cuisine. Earth-toned color palettes, cultural sun motifs, and balanced typography were used to evoke heritage and hospitality without overwhelming clarity.

The final design system supports both emotional connection and functional usability, ensuring menus and printed materials feel personal, inviting, and cohesive across all customer touchpoints.
Scope
Stationery Design and Menu Layout System
Deliverables
Trifold Brochure Front and Back Design, Label Stickers, Menu Design
Tools
Adobe Illustrator, InDesign, Photoshop
